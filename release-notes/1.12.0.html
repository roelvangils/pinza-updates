What's new

  • Power Peek. Hold ⌘⌥ in the popover to reveal the raw URL on every row and the ⌘1…⌘9 quick-pick chips at the same time. Hovered or focused rows scroll long URLs marquee-style so you can read them in full. New "Power Peek" toggle in Settings (on by default).
  • Standard hover tooltip. The hover tooltip now follows macOS conventions for placement and dwell.
  • Poof on delete. Removing an item from Recents plays the macOS poof animation next to the deleted row with a short pop sound. New toggle in Settings (on by default).
  • Multi-selection HUD. When the frontmost app has more than one item selected, Pinza shows a dedicated HUD telling you to pick one instead of copying the wrong link.
  • Non-English menu paths. Strategies that drive an app's menu now fall back to the keyboard shortcut when the localized menu title can't be resolved — fixes copy paths on non-English macOS.
  • SnippetsLab support. Copy a deep link to the currently-open snippet.
