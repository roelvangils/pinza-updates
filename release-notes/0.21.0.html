What's new

Window Styles — a new setting (Settings → General) lets you choose how Pinza shows your pins:

  • Popover — the menu-bar popover, now drawn with a pointy beak pointing right at the menu-bar icon.
  • Launcher — a centered, Raycast-style window: a search field on top, your pins on the left, and a live preview of the selected pin on the right.

Both open from the menu-bar icon and the global hotkey, and the Launcher now has full keyboard and mouse parity with the popover — the ⌘K actions menu, multi-select (⇧/⌘-click and shift-hover), reorder (⌘↑/⌘↓), group filtering, ⌘1–9 quick-pick, Power-Peek, and the complete keyboard-shortcut set all behave the same in both.

Also fixed

  • Detached preview Card: the close button, its fade-on-close, and a more physical tear-off.
